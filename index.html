BANARAS RUSH

Drive your auto-rickshaw through the old city. Pick up passengers, drop them at the ghats, and honk your way past the cows.

Hold one finger to drive. Drag left or right to steer, up for speed, down to brake. Tap a second finger to honk.

W / S to drive and brake, A / D to steer, Space to honk.
